Between 2011 and 2013 I kept a photography blog in French — film cameras,
street photography, photojournalism, and a long series from an internship at
Phaestos Presse. It moved off WordPress years ago and its old domain stopped
resolving; these posts are recovered here at their original URLs.

They are archived as written, in French, and are not representative of what I
work on now — for that, see [the blog](/blog/).
